# Citing

If you use SAGARI, please cite the paper that describes and validates the method:

> Waters, T. K., & Gültekin, K. 2026, *Schwarzschild Orbit-library Modeling with an Order of Magnitude Fewer
> Models: Adaptive Gaussian-process Sampling and a Case Study of M 86*, ApJ, submitted

and the software itself (`CITATION.cff` in the repository; the archived version on Zenodo).
